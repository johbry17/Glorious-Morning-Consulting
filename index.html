The Practice

A different kind of analysis

Glorious Morning Consulting is an independent practice. The work combines rigorous analytical methods with close attention to context: understanding what the question actually is, what the evidence can and cannot show, and how findings should be communicated to the people who will use them.

The principal brings a background spanning international affairs, environmental policy, sustainable development, and data science — a range of perspectives that shapes how questions are framed, how evidence is evaluated, and how answers are presented.
