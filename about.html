The practice

Glorious Morning Consulting is an independent data analysis, research, and visualization practice. It exists to help organizations that need rigorous analytical work done by someone who takes the subject seriously — not only the tools.

The work is done by the principal, who came to data science by what might generously be called a scenic route. The journey involved two master's degrees — in international affairs and development, and in environmental policy and natural resource management — along with years working in Caribbean hospitality, travel across five continents, and a sustained interest in how institutions make decisions about complex systems.

Before arriving at Python and PostgreSQL, the principal spent time studying sustainable tourism, working in hotel management, and developing a reasonably deep familiarity with what it looks like when data informs a decision and when it doesn't.

All of which is to say: the analytical work is grounded in something. The principal understands how a policy decision gets made, what a nonprofit needs from an analysis, why a community-level map matters differently from a regional one, and why the framing of a question changes the answer.

The approach

The question always comes before the dataset. The work is designed around understanding what decision needs to be made, what the evidence can and cannot show, and how findings should be communicated to the people who will use them — not to the people who produced them.

This means being honest about uncertainty, transparent about methodology, and attentive to the difference between what the data shows and what it implies. It also means understanding the real-world context: the policy environment, the institutional constraints, the communities involved, and the competing interpretations that any serious analysis will have to reckon with.

I read data like a scientist and a room like a bartender. Both skills turn out to be useful.

Orientation

The practice is oriented toward organizations with a genuine analytical question and a purpose behind it. Work in environmental policy, climate resilience, housing, community development, sustainable tourism, international development, and public-interest research is familiar territory.

The principal is based in Washington, D.C., and works remotely on project engagements.
